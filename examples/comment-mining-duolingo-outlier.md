# Comment report: [i'm literally not talking like anything](https://www.tiktok.com/@duolingo/video/7694371205087759647) (6.06M views)

Sources: 1 post, 100 comments read, about 86 after removing duplicates (the same comment ID came back twice in 7 cases), empty and image-only comments.

## What they keep asking

1. What the reference is. The joke depends on knowing the show; a share of viewers did not.
2. For their lost streaks back, under an entertainment post. Support demand shows up wherever the brand is.
3. For the app to teach more: "now actually teach me a language instead of telling me nonstop the word sushi when its the same in english💔"

## Objections and doubts

- The remake changed a character's look; about 6 comments call it out.
- Several comments name the original creators, asking for credit.

## Ideas

- When remaking a well-known scene, credit the original in the caption; the comments ask for it.
- Pin a reply that names the reference; it answers the "what is this" questions and keeps the thread going.
- Streak-recovery content (how to restore a streak) has demand even under unrelated posts.
- Merch signal: "someone invest in making real cucumbers with duo's face its too cute 🥹🥹"
