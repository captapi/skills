## What the outliers have in common

1. **Very short.** All 8 outliers run 7–17 seconds. The two long-form "duotv" episodes (45 and 84 seconds) sit at 0.3–0.4x the median.
2. **Borrowed context.** A TV reference, a collectible trend, a live event, an anime costume. The joke lands only if you know the reference, which pulls people into the comments.
3. **The streak as a running gag.** 3 of 8 outliers are streak jokes.
4. **Brand tie-ins do not break out.** The five Taco Bell posts (1–6 Oct) range from 199k to 604k views (0.4x–1.1x). None clears the bar.
