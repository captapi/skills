## Angles they rely on

- **Gymshark:** catalog best-sellers as the always-on base, a celebrity partner for reach, and a brand line ("We Do Gym").
- **Alphalete:** one hero product family (Pump) sold on fit and feel, creator posts with discount codes, and new-product drops tested with about 5 variants at once.

## What to test

1. A creator-style post with a personal discount code, left running for months if it holds. Alphalete's two April creator-style ads are among its longest-running ads, next to the Pump Mini Flare concept.
2. "Gym to street" positioning for a flagship product ("walking out of the gym", "train in and still wear out"); it appears in Alphalete's oldest and newest concepts.
3. Launch a product with about 5 ad variants in the same week, then keep what survives. Both brands do this (Gymshark's Megan Thee Stallion launch, Alphalete's Relay pant and Pump pushes).

Notes: public Meta Ad Library data. No spend, impressions or results for commercial ads. Days running is a proxy, not proof of performance.
